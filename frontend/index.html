Leaving the validator set?

Going offline for a long time? Revoke your finality key first.

These instructions are only for operators who intend to stop validating. Revoking removes your eligibility to produce staking blocks once the updated committee takes effect. This is not a recovery step. Your staked coins are NOT withdrawn.

  • Keep some spendable B3 for the transaction fee.
  • The revocation takes effect when the current committee handover completes — keep running until your validator has left the active committee.
  • Do not delete the finality_signer folder; revocation does not erase earlier signatures.
These features run without you

Automation needs your wallet passphrase stored on this machine so it can act while you are away. That is a real trade-off: convenience for unattended operation, against the risk that anyone with access to this server's data directory could use it. Only enable it on hardware you control.

Keep staking after a restart

After a reboot or crash your node normally stops staking until you unlock the wallet by hand. With this on, it tops your stake back up to a target on its own.

FN Coin

FN Coins are created, not mined. Each one is made by permanently destroying a fixed amount of B3 — the supply is capped for the life of the chain, and the price rises as more are made.

The B3 is destroyed, not spent

Creating an FN Coin burns the B3 out of existence. It is not sent to anyone, not held in escrow, and cannot be refunded or recovered by anybody.

Only one FN Coin can be created at a time across the whole network. If someone else gets the current slot, your attempt is refused and you simply try again after the next block — nothing is lost.

Encrypt your access This connection is currently plain HTTP
Not encrypted

On this kind of connection, everything — including your password — travels unencrypted. (Localhost and HTTPS connections are safe: the former never leaves the machine, the latter is encrypted, and both additionally seal your password and passphrase with built-in envelope encryption.) To fix this connection, pick one of these — easiest first:

Option 1 — Tailscale (recommended, 10 minutes, no domain): Install the free Tailscale app on this device and on the machine running B3 Hive, sign in with the same account, then open the https:// address the app gives you. Your connection gets real encryption with a real certificate, and the wallet stops being reachable from the public internet.
Option 2 — Reverse proxy with your own domain: If you own a domain, point it at this machine and run the bundled Caddy profile (see docs/ARCHITECTURE.md). Caddy obtains and renews a Let's Encrypt certificate automatically.

Already have HTTPS in front? Your operator can set REQUIRE_SECURE_TRANSPORT=block to refuse all plain-HTTP remote logins outright.
